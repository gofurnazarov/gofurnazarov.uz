PARENT + CHILDREN CONVERSATION PRODUCT — LOCKED V1 PLAN
=============================================================

Status: READY FOR EXECUTION
Date: 8 September 2026

1. PRODUCT TARGET
-----------------
Customer:
- Parents with children aged 6–12.

Child age constraint:
- Questions must be simple enough for a 6-year-old to understand.
- Questions must remain interesting enough for a 12-year-old.
- We will NOT split the age range for V1.
- Later, actual customer data can determine whether separate packs are needed
  (for example 6–8, 9–12, or 13–17).

2. CORE PROBLEM
---------------
The parent wants to spend meaningful time with their child but often does not
know what to talk about.

The product removes the need for the parent to think of conversation topics.

Core job:
"Help parents have meaningful conversations with their children without
having to think of what to talk about."

3. DESIRED OUTCOME
------------------
Emotional outcome:
"Men farzandimni yaxshiroq tushuna boshladim."

Behavioral outcome:
"Har kuni 10–15 daqiqa telefonni chetga qo‘yib, farzandim bilan gaplashdim."

Core product promise:
"Har kuni 10 daqiqalik suhbat.
Farzandingizni yaxshiroq tanish uchun 100 ta savol."

Important:
- The product is NOT positioned as therapy.
- It is NOT positioned as parenting education.
- It is NOT positioned as psychological assessment.
- It is NOT simply "100 interesting questions."

We are selling:
DISCOVERY + CONNECTION + FUN.

4. PRODUCT PHILOSOPHY
---------------------
"Don't interview your child. Have a conversation with them."

The product should help create a two-way interaction between parent and child,
rather than making the parent mechanically ask 100 questions.

Some questions should naturally invite the parent to answer too.

Examples of useful structures:
- Parent answers first, then child.
- Child answers, then parent shares their own experience.
- A question naturally creates a follow-up question.

5. PRODUCT FORMAT
-----------------
Format:
- Digital only.
- Interactive Telegram Web App.

V1 should remain extremely simple.

Core experience:
OPEN → GET QUESTION → TALK → NEXT QUESTION

The conversation happens between the parent and child, not inside the app.

Avoid unnecessary V1 features:
- Complex accounts
- Profiles
- AI chat
- Scores
- Points
- Leaderboards
- Excessive gamification
- Complicated progress systems

Optional UX:
- "Bugungi savol"
- Random question
- Category selection

Categories are primarily an editorial/content structure. The product should
still feel like a simple conversation experience rather than a questionnaire
database.

6. FIRST PACK
-------------
First product:
100 parent-child conversation questions.

Initial editorial categories:

1. ❤️ Bir-birimizni yaxshiroq bilamiz — 20
2. 😂 Kulamiz va tasavvur qilamiz — 20
3. 🏠 Oilamiz haqida — 15
4. 🌱 Orzular va kelajak — 15
5. 🧠 Fikrlar va qarashlar — 15
6. 💭 Yurakdan suhbat — 15

TOTAL: 100

Important:
These categories organize the content. They do not mean every question has
to fit only one emotional function.

7. QUESTION PURPOSES
--------------------
Every question should accomplish at least one of these:

❤️ CONNECTION
Create warmth and emotional closeness.

🧠 UNDERSTANDING
Reveal what the child thinks, feels, notices, or values.

🌱 SELF-DISCOVERY
Help the child articulate personality, preferences, values, or identity.

🚀 DREAMS & IMAGINATION
Open up aspirations, creativity, and hypothetical thinking.

😂 FUN
Make the experience enjoyable and playful.

👨‍👩‍👧 FAMILY
Create conversation around family, home, memories, and relationships.

💭 DEEPER CONVERSATION
Occasionally move beyond surface-level conversation without becoming
therapeutic or emotionally unsafe.

The collection must balance these purposes.

8. QUESTION-WRITING RULES
-------------------------
RULE 1 — Age appropriate
Every question must be understandable to a 6-year-old while remaining
interesting to a 12-year-old.

RULE 2 — Open-ended
Prefer questions that invite explanation, stories, opinions, imagination,
feelings, or discovery.

Avoid yes/no questions unless there is a very strong reason.

RULE 3 — No obvious correct answer
The question should not feel like a test or force a socially desirable answer.

RULE 4 — Trigger meaningful conversation
A strong question should trigger at least one of:
- Story
- Imagination
- Opinion
- Feeling
- Discovery

RULE 5 — High follow-up potential
A good answer should naturally make the parent want to ask:
"Why?", "How?", "What happened next?", or another related question.

The question should not kill the conversation.

RULE 6 — Do not interrogate
The experience must feel like a game/conversation, not an interview or
parenting homework.

Therefore, mix:
- Easy
- Funny
- Curious
- Personal
- Imaginative
- Meaningful
- Light
- Deeper

Do not arrange the entire pack as a steady escalation into increasingly
serious questions.

RULE 7 — Include the parent
Some questions should deliberately create two-way sharing.

The parent should sometimes answer first or answer after the child.

RULE 8 — No forced vulnerability
Do not pressure children to reveal trauma, private information, serious fears,
family conflict, or painful experiences.

Avoid questions such as:
- "Who hurt you the most?"
- "What do you hate about your parents?"
- "What is your biggest fear?"

Prefer emotionally meaningful but safe alternatives.

RULE 9 — Natural Uzbek
Questions must sound like something an Uzbek parent would naturally say to
their child.

Do NOT simply translate Western conversation-card questions.

Use familiar contexts when appropriate:
- Oila
- Maktab
- Do'stlar
- Buvi/buva
- Mahalla
- Ta'til
- Bayramlar
- Uy
- Kundalik hayot
- Orzular
- Kelajak

But avoid making the questions culturally narrow or boring.

RULE 10 — No repetition
Do not create multiple versions of the same question.

For example, these are effectively duplicates:
- "Katta bo'lganingda kim bo'lmoqchisan?"
- "Kelajakda nima qilmoqchisan?"
- "Qaysi kasbni tanlaysan?"
- "Orzu qilgan kasbing qaysi?"

Each final question must have a distinct conversational purpose.

RULE 11 — Parent reaction test
Ask:
"If my child gave me an unexpected answer, would I genuinely want to hear it?"

Prefer questions that can reveal something the parent may not already know.

Avoid low-discovery questions unless they serve an important fun purpose.

RULE 12 — Natural difficulty/depth curve
The collection should move naturally between light and meaningful conversation.

Do not make the entire pack deep.
Do not make the entire pack silly.

The product should feel:
FUN FIRST, MEANINGFUL SECOND.

9. WHAT WE ARE NOT DOING
------------------------
We explicitly removed the "Instagram test."

A question does NOT need to be good Instagram content to belong in the
product.

Marketing criteria and product-quality criteria are separate.

A question's only job is to make the conversation/product better.

10. QUALITY STANDARD
--------------------
Every question should ideally pass all of these:

- Understandable to a 6-year-old
- Interesting to a 12-year-old
- Open-ended
- No obvious correct answer
- Produces story/opinion/feeling/imagination/discovery
- Has natural follow-up potential
- Parent genuinely wants the answer
- Does not feel like interrogation
- Natural Uzbek
- Not redundant
- Appropriate for a parent-child setting
- Safe and non-invasive
- Distinct conversational purpose

If a question fails multiple criteria, DELETE IT.

Do not keep weak questions just to reach 100.

11. CONTENT PRODUCTION PROCESS
------------------------------
Do NOT immediately generate exactly 100 questions and accept them.

Step 1:
Generate approximately 140–160 candidate questions.

Step 2:
Organize them according to the six editorial categories.

Step 3:
Perform a ruthless editorial pass.

Delete questions that are:
- Generic
- Repetitive
- Childish
- Too abstract
- Too difficult
- Too obvious
- Too emotionally invasive
- Boring
- Unnatural in Uzbek
- Unlikely to create discussion
- Merely variations of stronger questions

Step 4:
Replace deleted questions with stronger candidates.

Step 5:
Review all surviving questions TOGETHER, not individually.

This is essential because repetition is easiest to detect at collection level.

Step 6:
Finalize the strongest 100.

If only 83 questions are genuinely excellent, do not fill the remaining
17 with garbage. Quality comes before the number 100.

12. CONTENT ARCHITECTURE
------------------------
Target balance:

30% — Fun & imagination
25% — Getting to know the child
20% — Family & relationship
15% — Dreams & opinions
10% — Deeper emotional conversation

This is a guideline, not a mathematical prison.

The final collection should feel varied rather than mechanically balanced.

13. LANGUAGE / STYLE
--------------------
Language:
- Uzbek
- Natural conversational Uzbek
- Warm
- Simple
- Playful
- Human

Avoid:
- Corporate language
- Psychology jargon
- Educational jargon
- Artificial motivational language
- Overly poetic wording
- Adult vocabulary
- Formal textbook phrasing

The parent should be able to read the question aloud naturally.

14. V1 VALIDATION
-----------------
We are not going to spend weeks researching before testing.

Execution philosophy:
BUILD → SELL → LEARN

Initial V1:
- 100 questions
- Simple Telegram Web App
- Digital-only
- Minimal UI
- Simple payment flow

Initial acquisition test:
- Approximately $3 Instagram test
- Measure actual purchases, not merely clicks

The $3 test can tell us whether there is an initial willingness to pay.
It cannot prove scalability.

Interpretation:
- No clicks → creative/targeting problem
- Clicks but no checkout → offer/product presentation problem
- Checkout but no payment → price/trust/payment friction problem
- Purchases → promising signal; iterate and test again

Do not make major conclusions from one tiny test.

15. INITIAL PRODUCT STRATEGY
----------------------------
We are choosing Parent + Children as the first niche because:

- The underlying problem is clear.
- Parents have a strong emotional reason to spend time with their children.
- The mechanism is simple.
- The product is inexpensive to create.
- Digital delivery has almost zero marginal fulfillment cost.
- Telegram Web App is a natural interactive format.
- The concept can later expand into additional relationship niches.

Potential future packs:
- Couples
- Family
- Mother + daughter
- Father + son
- Friends
- Teenagers
- Other relationship/conversation formats

But NONE of these are part of V1.

Focus on proving the first product.
